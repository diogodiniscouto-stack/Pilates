About Us

A brand built on movement and form

Base Movement was founded on the belief that Pilates equipment can, and should, be as thoughtfully designed as the space that houses it.

Founded by design and movement professionals, Base Movement is dedicated to creating Pilates equipment that unites technical rigour, noble materials and a serene visual language.

We believe a well-equipped studio is also a space of trust, for those who run it, and for those who move within it.

01

Mission

To elevate the standard of professional Pilates equipment, uniting technical rigour with Scandinavian design sensibility.

02

Vision

To be Europe's reference in premium Pilates equipment, recognised for quality, durability and aesthetic coherence.

03

Craftsmanship

Every piece is produced by selected manufacturing partners, combining artisanal process with precision engineering.

04

Design Philosophy

We draw inspiration from Scandinavian architecture and the attention to detail of fine watchmaking: simple forms, honest materials, nothing superfluous.

05

Certified Quality

Certified production, with responsibly sourced materials and quality control at every stage of the process.

An invitation to mindful movement

Every Base Movement piece is designed to last, in structure, material and taste. We invite you to explore the collection and imagine your next studio.
